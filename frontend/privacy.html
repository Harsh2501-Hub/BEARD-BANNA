Privacy Policy & Data Sovereignty

Effective Date: November 2026 | Last Updated: November 2026
Brand: BEARD BANNA | Tagline: HONOR • HISTORY • HERITAGE

⚜ Sacred Covenant with Our Patrons:
At BEARD BANNA, we revere your personal sovereignty and privacy as paramount. This policy provides full, transparent disclosure of how customer information is gathered, utilized, safeguarded, and governed across our online boutique (beard-banna.vercel.app) in compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and applicable international privacy benchmarks.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential personal information required to execute your orders, deliver royal customer care, and provide an exquisite online shopping journey:

  • Account & Registration Details: When you join the House of Beard Banna, we collect your full name, email address, optional contact telephone number, and encrypted authentication credentials. Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed; we never possess or store plaintext passwords.
  • Shipping & Delivery Information: To dispatch your bespoke Rajputana garments, we collect recipient name, physical delivery address, city, state, PIN code, and mobile phone number for logistics verification.
  • Tax & Invoicing Data: For statutory compliance under India's Goods and Services Tax (GST) laws, we compute applicable CGST, SGST, or IGST based on destination state. For business patrons, optional GSTIN identifiers are retained strictly for formal tax invoices.
  • Payment Transaction Data: Payments processed online are facilitated securely via certified RBI-authorized payment aggregators (such as Razorpay). BEARD BANNA never stores or receives your debit/credit card numbers, CVV security codes, net banking passwords, or UPI PINs. All financial transactions are tokenized end-to-end.
  • Customer Support & Royal Inquiries: Any correspondence submitted via our Royal Inquiry Desk, including inquiry subjects, messages, and contact details, is retained to resolve order queries and support tickets.
  • Customer Reviews & Imagery: Voluntary reviews, star ratings, and optional user-submitted apparel photographs are published only with explicit patron submission.
  • Browsing & Analytical Data: With your permission, aggregated device identifiers, approximate geographic region, page engagement, and referral channels are measured via privacy-conscious Google Analytics 4 (GA4).

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Your personal data is processed strictly for legitimate, declared purposes:

  • Fulfilling orders, tailoring logistics, and dispatching shipping confirmations and tracking updates.
  • Authenticating customer access to personal order histories, addresses, and wishlists.
  • Generating legally mandated GST tax invoices in compliance with Indian commercial statutes.
  • Preventing bot attacks, spam, fraudulent transactions, and safeguarding system integrity.
  • Continuously refining the royal design, fit, and speed of our online boutique.

4. Cookie Governance & Preferences

We employ cookies to maintain your active shopping cart, remember session preferences, and understand website performance. In accordance with India's DPDP Act and international privacy frameworks, you have sovereign control over non-essential analytical cookies:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for browsing, secure login sessions, and maintaining your cart. These cannot be disabled as the boutique cannot function without them.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Activated only when you select "Accept All" on our cookie consent portal. You may adjust or revoke your consent at any moment by clicking the button below.

5. Data Security & Storage

We maintain robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All web traffic is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/SSL (HTTPS) 256-bit encryption.
  • Database Isolation: Data stored in our cloud infrastructure utilizes Row-Level Security (RLS) policies to ensure patrons can only view and update their own authenticated records.
  • Access Control: Administrative access is restricted to authorized personnel via multi-factor authentication and role-based privilege tiers.

6. Your Sovereign Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

Under applicable Indian data protection laws and international privacy regulations, you possess the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request a summary of personal data held about you and processing activities undertaken.
  • Right to Correction & Updating: You may update inaccurate or outdated profile and shipping details at any time through your Customer Profile.
  • Right to Erasure / Account Deletion: You may request complete erasure of your personal data, subject to mandatory statutory tax and accounting retention requirements (e.g. GST invoices must be retained as mandated by Indian law).
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You have the right to register concerns or complaints regarding your personal data with our designated Grievance Officer.
