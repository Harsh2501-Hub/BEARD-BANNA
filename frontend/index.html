Frequently Asked Questions

⚜

Everything you need to know regarding placing your orders, Rajputana craftsmanship, tailored fit, Pan-India delivery, and royal customer care.

Ordering your bespoke heritage apparel is effortless. Visit our Heritage Collection, select your desired design (Polo, Round Neck, or Hoodie), choose your tailored size (S to XXL), and click "Add to Cart". When you are ready, proceed to Checkout, provide your delivery address, and choose between Cash on Delivery (COD) or online payment via UPI, Debit/Credit Card, or Net Banking.

We provide multiple bank-grade, PCI-DSS compliant payment channels powered by Razorpay:

  • Cash on Delivery (COD) — Available across serviceable PIN codes in India.
  • UPI Instant Payments — Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and any banking UPI handle.
  • Credit & Debit Cards — Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, and American Express.
  • Net Banking — Supported across 50+ major Indian banks.

The House of BEARD BANNA ships Pan-India across all 28 states and union territories. Standard shipping is transparently calculated during checkout based on your state. Orders above ₹999 qualify for promotional Free Express Shipping across India.

Each garment is inspected and packed with royal care. Orders are dispatched from our hub in Rajasthan within 24 to 48 business hours. Typical delivery timelines range from 4 to 7 business days depending on your destination city, state logistics, and courier connectivity.

Upon placing an order, you will receive a unique Order ID (e.g. BB12345678). You can track its live timeline and fulfillment stage anytime by visiting our Track Order service or logging into your Customer Profile.

Orders may be cancelled or modified before they are dispatched from our fulfillment center. To request an urgent cancellation or address update, please contact our team via WhatsApp at +91 9586479121 or submit an inquiry with your Order ID through the contact portal below.

We take profound pride in our craftsmanship and want you to wear your heritage apparel with total confidence. If a garment does not fit as desired or arrives damaged, you may initiate a size exchange or return within 7 days of delivery.

* Note: Items must be unwashed, unworn, and returned in their original packaging with all royal tags intact. (Exact return terms subject to final brand confirmation).

Our garments are tailored to a comfortable Regular Royal Fit using 100% premium breathable cotton. We provide an interactive Size Guide modal on every product page showing exact Chest, Length, and Shoulder measurements in inches and cm. If you prefer a relaxed or streetwear drape, we suggest choosing one size larger.
